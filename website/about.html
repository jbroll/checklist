About BubbleList

BubbleList is a checklist app designed for lists you use again and again. Unlike traditional checklist apps, checking items off doesn't modify your list—progress is tracked separately in sessions, so your list is always ready for the next use.

Perfect for grocery shopping, packing lists, daily routines, and any recurring checklist.
