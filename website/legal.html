Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Email address (via Google or Apple OAuth)
  • Authentication tokens

List Data

Your list data includes:

  • Lists, folders, categories, and items you create
  • Sessions and item states
  • Organization structure and metadata

Your list data is stored using Jazz.tools. Jazz account keys are stored server-side, which means we can technically access your data if required by law.

We collect minimal usage data:

  • Device type and browser information

What We Don't Collect

  • Third-party advertising trackers

  • Fingerprinting data

How We Use Your Information

  • Store and sync your lists across devices
  • Authenticate your account
  • Fix bugs and improve the service
  • Respond to support requests

Data Storage

BubbleList uses Jazz.tools for storage:

  • Data stored locally on your device first
  • Encrypted during sync
  • Cloud backup via Jazz.tools

Security

  • HTTPS/TLS for all communications
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication via BetterAuth
  • CSRF protection

Data Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Auth data removed immediately
  • List data deleted immediately from your account
  • Encryption keys destroyed immediately
  • Local device data cleared immediately

Technical note: BubbleList uses end-to-end encryption via Jazz.tools. When you delete your account, your encryption keys are destroyed, making any data cryptographically inaccessible. Encrypted data fragments may temporarily remain on sync servers but cannot be read without your deleted keys.
