Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2025

Our Commitment to Privacy

At BubbleList, privacy isn't just a feature—it's our foundation. We built this application with privacy and data ownership at its core. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you sign up for BubbleList, we collect:

  • Email address (via OAuth providers: Google, Apple)
  • Name and profile picture (from OAuth provider, if provided)
  • Authentication tokens for secure access

2. List Data

Your list data includes:

  • Templates, folders, categories, and items you create
  • Shopping sessions and item states
  • Organization structure and metadata

Important: Your list data is stored using Jazz.tools encrypted storage. Jazz account keys are stored server-side for convenience. This means we can technically access your data if required by law, but we never access it for any other purpose.

3. Usage Information

We collect minimal usage information for improving the service:

  • Device type and browser information
  • General usage patterns (pages visited, features used)
  • Error logs and crash reports

We use privacy-friendly analytics tools and do not track individual user behavior across the web.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers
  • We do not collect browsing history outside our application
  • We do not use fingerprinting or similar tracking technologies
  • We do not sell or share your data for marketing purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide the service: Store and sync your lists across devices
  • To authenticate you: Verify your identity and secure your account
  • To improve the service: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
  • To communicate with you: Send important service updates and respond to support requests
  • To ensure security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Local-First Architecture

BubbleList uses a local-first architecture powered by Jazz.tools:

  • Local storage: Your data is stored on your device
  • Encrypted sync: Data is encrypted during transmission and storage
  • Peer-to-peer sync: Direct device-to-device sync when possible
  • Cloud backup: Encrypted cloud storage via Jazz.tools for sync and backup

Security Measures

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all communications
  • Secure authentication via BetterAuth with OAuth 2.0
  • CSRF protection and secure cookies
  • Regular security audits and updates

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your list data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Authentication data will be removed immediately
  • Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Jazz.tools: Encrypted data storage and sync (see Jazz Privacy Policy)
  • Google OAuth: Authentication service (see Google Privacy Policy)
  • Apple Sign In: Authentication service (see Apple Privacy Policy)

When We May Share Data

We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Protection: To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users
  • Business transfer: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified)

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export: Export your list data in JSON, CSV, or TXT format at any time
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications (if any)

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@bubblelist.com.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies only for essential functionality:

  • Authentication cookies: Keep you logged in securely
  • Session cookies: Maintain your session across pages

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Children's Privacy

BubbleList is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

International Data Transfers

BubbleList is operated in the United States. If you access the service from outside the US, your data may be transferred to and stored in the US. By using BubbleList, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of BubbleList after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We built BubbleList because we believe privacy matters. We're committed to transparency, honesty, and protecting your data. If you have any questions or concerns, we're here to listen.
