FAQ

How does checking things off work?

Your list never changes when you check things off. Each use starts a new session that tracks progress separately.

How does offline mode work?

All data lives on your device first. Create, edit, check items offline. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Not stored as plaintext on servers.

Can I share lists with others?

Yes! Share folders via email. Real-time sync with view-only or full edit permissions.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export to JSON, CSV, or TXT anytime. No vendor lock-in.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! It's a PWA—works in any browser, installs to home screen, full offline support.

Is it free?

Free during beta. No ads, no data harvesting.

How is this different from other apps?

Most apps modify your list when you check off items. BubbleList keeps your list separate from progress—always ready for next use.
