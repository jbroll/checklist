Frequently Asked Questions

How does checking things off work?

Your list never changes when you check things off. Instead, each time you use a list (like a grocery run or packing for a trip), you start a new session. The session tracks your progress. When you're done, your list is ready for next time—no resetting needed.

How does offline mode work?

BubbleList is built on Jazz.tools, a local-first database. All your data lives on your device first. You can create, edit, and check items completely offline. When you reconnect, changes sync automatically using conflict-free merge technology.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Jazz.tools' cryptographic layer. Your data isn't stored as plaintext on sync servers. However, this is group-based encryption (for sharing), not zero-knowledge end-to-end encryption like Signal.

Can I share lists with others?

Yes! Share folders via email invite. Recipients get real-time sync of all templates and sessions in that folder. You control permissions—view-only or full edit access.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export templates and sessions to JSON, CSV, or TXT formats anytime. Your data, your choice. No vendor lock-in.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! BubbleList is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Works in any browser and can be installed to your home screen for an app-like experience with full offline support.

Is it free?

Yes, currently free during beta. No ads, no data harvesting. Future pricing (if any) will focus on optional premium features while keeping core functionality accessible.

How is this different from AnyList, OurGroceries, etc.?

Most list apps modify your list when you check things off. You have to uncheck everything to use it again. BubbleList keeps your list separate from your progress—so your list is always ready for the next use. Plus: nested folders, nested categories, encrypted sync, and no ads.
