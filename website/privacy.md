# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: July 23, 2026*

**When you sign up,** we collect your email address via your chosen sign-in provider.

**Your list data** includes everything you create in the app: lists, folders, categories, items, sessions, and their organization. Your list data is stored on our sync servers so it stays in sync across your devices and can be shared with people you invite. It is not end-to-end encrypted, which means we can technically access it; we only do so if required by law or as described below.

You can export your lists anytime (JSON, CSV, TXT) or delete your account.

**When you delete your account,** your authentication credentials are deleted immediately, which permanently revokes all access to your synced data. Local data is cleared immediately on the device that initiates account deletion, but may remain on other devices until they are signed out or cleared.

**Tracking and cookies.** No analytics, telemetry, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting. We only use cookies for authentication and session management.

**How we operate the service.** We use your email to authenticate your account and respond to support requests. Your list data is stored locally on your device first and synced to our servers over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection. All communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption.

**Third-party services.** We use third-party services for data sync and OAuth authentication. Your use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

**We share data only when** required by law, necessary to protect our rights or users' safety, or part of a business transfer (with notice). We don't sell your data.

**International users.** {{brand.name}} operates in the United States. Using the service means your data may be transferred to the US.
