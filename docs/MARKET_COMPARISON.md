# Market Comparison: List Apps

A competitive analysis of the list/checklist app market and CheckList's positioning.

*Last updated: December 2025*

---

## Major Competitors

### AnyList
**Price**: Free / $9.99/year individual / $14.99/year household

The market leader for families and meal planners. Near-perfect 4.9 App Store rating.

**Free Tier**:
- Create and share multiple lists
- Real-time sync between devices
- Auto-categorization by store aisle
- Add items via Siri voice
- Store recipes (manual entry or copy/paste)
- Barcode scanning

**Premium (AnyList Complete) Adds**:
- Web and desktop access (Mac, Windows)
- Recipe import from websites (Allrecipes, Food Network, etc.)
- Browser extensions for recipe saving
- Meal planning calendar
- Recipe scaling
- Color-coded folders
- Custom themes (fonts, colors, textures)
- Location-based reminders
- Apple Watch app
- Item photos
- Passcode lock
- Price tracking

**Template/List Model**:
No true templates. Lists are live documents—checking items moves them to "crossed off" section. Unchecking brings them back. Auto-complete suggests previously-used items. Users create recurring lists by unchecking items each week.

**Limitations**: Proprietary, data stored on their servers unencrypted

---

### OurGroceries
**Price**: Free with ads / $1/month / $6/year / $20 lifetime

Established since 2009, focused on simplicity and family sharing.

**Free Tier** (all features, with ads):
- Real-time shared lists across all platforms
- Barcode scanning and photo attachments
- Recipe storage with one-tap ingredient addition
- Alexa integration
- Apple Watch support
- Remembers frequently purchased items

**Premium Adds**:
- Ad-free experience (for entire household)
- That's it—all features are free

**Template/List Model**:
No templates. Lists persist with items. Checking moves to bottom, unchecking restores. App remembers item history and suggests as you type. Recipes can add ingredients to lists with one tap.

**Limitations**: Basic organization (flat lists only), dated interface

---

### Bring!
**Price**: Free with ads / $1.99/month / $8.99/year

Visual-first approach with colorful icons instead of text.

**Free Tier**:
- Visual item icons for quick recognition
- Shared lists with real-time notifications
- Voice input and smart search
- Item descriptions, quantities, photos
- Auto-grouping by category (customizable)
- Recipe discovery and import from websites
- Store loyalty card wallet
- Dark/light mode, tile/list view

**Premium Adds**:
- Ad-free experience
- Custom themes and list customization
- Priority customer support

**Template/List Model**:
Pre-made occasion lists (brunch, garden party, BBQ) serve as templates. No user-created templates. Items persist on lists; checking removes them visually. "Your items" section remembers everything you've added.

**Limitations**: Visual icons not for everyone, no true template separation

---

### Cozi
**Price**: Free with ads / $39/year Gold

Full family organizer, not just shopping lists.

**Free Tier**:
- Shared shopping lists (sorted by category)
- Family calendar
- To-do lists
- Up to 12 family members
- Basic reminders

**Premium (Cozi Gold) Adds**:
- Ad-free experience for entire family
- Mobile month view calendar
- Add/edit events 30+ days in advance
- Calendar search
- Calendar change notifications
- Up to 3 reminders per appointment
- Birthday tracker
- Text reminders for appointments
- Home screen widgets
- Priority support

**Template/List Model**:
No templates. Lists are live documents with items sorted by category. Checking crosses off items. No recipe or meal planning integration.

**Limitations**: Expensive ($39/yr), shopping lists are secondary to calendar features

---

### Listonic
**Price**: Free

AI-powered smart suggestions.

**Free Tier** (no premium tier):
- AI auto-suggestions while typing
- Voice input
- Auto-categorization
- Real-time collaboration
- Cross-platform sync

**Template/List Model**:
No explicit templates. AI suggests items based on history. Lists persist with checked/unchecked states.

**Limitations**: Less established, fewer integrations, limited documentation

---

### Out of Milk
**Price**: Free with ads

Unique pantry tracking focus.

**Free Tier** (no premium tier):
- Barcode scanner for pantry inventory
- Track "low/full" stock levels
- Running price totals while shopping
- Coupon and quantity notes
- List sharing

**Template/List Model**:
Pantry-centric approach. Your pantry IS your template—items with low stock auto-suggest for shopping lists. No separate template concept.

**Limitations**: Ad-supported only, simpler sharing, dated interface

---

### Todoist
**Price**: Free / $4/month / $6/month (Business)

General task manager with grocery template.

**Free Tier**:
- Up to 5 active projects
- Basic task management
- 5 collaborators per project
- 1-week activity history

**Premium Adds**:
- Unlimited projects
- Reminders
- Calendar integration
- Filters and labels
- 36+ integrations
- Unlimited activity history

**Template/List Model**:
Offers a grocery list template with categories (Produce, Dairy, etc.). Fully customizable. Tasks (items) can recur. Projects serve as reusable structures, but checking items completes them—you duplicate the project/template for each trip.

**Limitations**: Not purpose-built for shopping, overkill complexity

---

### Privacy Friendly Shopping List
**Price**: Free (Open Source)

Privacy-focused, available on F-Droid.

**Features**:
- Minimal permissions (camera only)
- All data stays local on device
- Open source code
- No tracking, no ads

**Template/List Model**:
Local lists only. No sync, no sharing, no templates.

**Limitations**: No sync, no collaboration—privacy through isolation

## How Competitors Handle "Templates"

| App | Template Approach | Reusability |
|-----|-------------------|-------------|
| **AnyList** | None. Lists are live. Uncheck to "reset" | Manual uncheck each week |
| **OurGroceries** | None. Item history suggests past purchases | Auto-suggest only |
| **Bring!** | Pre-made occasion lists (read-only) | Copy to your list |
| **Cozi** | None. Lists persist with checks | Manual management |
| **Listonic** | None. AI suggests from history | Auto-suggest only |
| **Out of Milk** | Pantry as implicit template | Stock levels trigger suggestions |
| **Todoist** | Project templates (duplicate to use) | Copy entire project |
| **CheckList** | **True templates with separate sessions** | **Session tracks state, template unchanged** |

**Key Insight**: No major competitor separates template content from shopping state. CheckList's template-session model is genuinely unique.

## CheckList Differentiators

### 1. Encrypted Data Sync

**What CheckList offers**: Data is encrypted using Jazz.tools' cryptographic layer. All data is signed with your account's keypair and encrypted with group read keys before leaving your device.

**Important caveat**: This is NOT zero-knowledge end-to-end encryption. Jazz uses a group-based encryption model where:
- Data is encrypted at rest on servers
- Encryption keys are managed through Jazz's group system
- The server operator (or Jazz Cloud) holds the key material needed to add members to groups
- Account keys are stored with BetterAuth (in SQLite, potentially unencrypted)

**Honest positioning**: "Encrypted sync" rather than "end-to-end encrypted." Better than plaintext storage (most competitors), but not Signal-level zero-knowledge.

**Why it still matters**:
- Data isn't sitting in plaintext on servers
- Requires explicit access grants through Jazz groups
- Better than most competitors who store everything readable

**Competitors**: Most store data in plaintext. Only "Privacy Friendly Shopping List" offers stronger privacy (but with no sync at all).

---

### 2. True Offline-First Architecture

**What it means**: Full functionality without internet. Create, edit, check items, complete sessions—all offline. Syncs automatically when reconnected.

**Why it matters**:
- Works in stores with poor cell reception
- No waiting for server responses
- Your data is always available
- Conflict resolution is automatic

**Competitors**: Most apps cache data but may require connectivity for certain operations. Jazz's CRDT-based sync is genuinely robust.

---

### 3. Template-Session Separation (Unique)

**What it means**: Templates are your master lists. Sessions track shopping progress. Checking off "Milk" in a session doesn't modify your template.

**Why it matters**:
- Reuse the same template week after week without resetting
- Compare sessions over time
- Never accidentally delete items from your master list
- Multiple people can shop different sessions simultaneously
- Session history persists for reference

**Competitors**: ALL major apps mix list content with shopping state. This is genuinely unique to CheckList.

---

### 4. Hierarchical Organization

**What it means**:
- **Folders**: Unlimited nesting (e.g., `Stores → Wegmans → Weekly`)
- **Categories**: Tree structure within templates (e.g., `Produce → Fruits → Berries`)

**Why it matters**:
- Organize by store, occasion, person, or any system
- Categories can contain sub-categories
- Scale from simple to complex without changing tools

**Competitors**: Most offer flat lists or single-level categories. Todoist has projects/sub-projects but isn't shopping-focused. AnyList has folders but only in premium.

---

### 5. No Ads, No Data Harvesting

**What it means**:
- No ads, ever (not even in free tier)
- No behavioral tracking
- No selling data to third parties
- Minimal permissions required

**Why it matters**: "Free" apps often monetize your shopping data. Your purchase patterns are valuable to advertisers, insurers, and data brokers.

**Competitors**: Most free tiers are ad-supported. Listonic is the only other major app without ads in free tier.

| Feature | Status | Why It Matters |
|---------|:------:|---------------|

| True template reuse | ✅ **Unique** | No competitor separates template from session state. |

| Item quantity | ✅ | `defaultQuantity` field with UI in ItemInput. |
| Item notes | ✅ | Template + session-level notes with modal editor. |

| Multiple view modes | ✅ | `zone-in-hierarchy` and `flat` modes exist. |

| **Autocomplete suggestions** | ✅ | Multi-domain search with keyboard navigation. |
| Search/filtering | ❌ | Filter items by name across templates. Zero schema changes needed. |

### What's in Schema vs Exposed in UI

CheckList's data model is already richer than most competitors. The opportunity is **exposing existing features**, not adding schema complexity.

| Schema Field | Location | Currently Exposed | Opportunity |
|--------------|----------|:-----------------:|-------------|
| `defaultQuantity` | TemplateItem | Partial | Show inline: "Milk (2 gal)" |
| `notes` | TemplateItem | Partial | Expandable detail or tooltip |
| `notes` | ItemState (session) | Partial | Quick inline edit while shopping |
| `selectedAt` | ItemState | ❌ | Session analytics |
| `checkedAt` | ItemState | ❌ | "Completed in 23 min" stats |
| `lastActivityAt` | SessionData | ❌ | "Last used 3 days ago" |
| `viewMode` | SessionData | ✅ | Already working |

### Tier 3: Features Requiring New Schema

These require schema additions. Most are project-management scope creep.

| Feature | Schema Impact | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| Tags/labels | New array field | Skip—hierarchy already provides organization |
| Reminders | Date field + notification system | Skip—significant complexity |
| Images | URL/blob storage | Skip—not core use case |
| Flexible permissions | Role fields | Consider later for team features |
| Automations | Trigger/action definitions | Skip—PM territory |

### Tier 4: Skip Entirely

These add complexity without proportional value for a list app:

- Gamification (streaks, points)
- Barcode scanning
- Voice assistant integrations
- Price tracking
- Recipe import
- Meal planning
- Kanban/calendar views

## Strategic Approach: Simplicity Over Features

Many "missing features" come from **project management apps** (Notion, ClickUp, Todoist), not list apps. CheckList should resist this scope creep.

**Philosophy**: Do lists exceptionally well, not everything adequately.

### High-Value, Zero-Schema Priorities

1. **Search** - Filter items by name across all templates
2. **Expose quantity inline** - Already stored, just show it
3. **Quick notes access** - Tap to see/edit without modal
4. **Session stats** - Use existing timestamps for completion time
5. **Smart input** - AI parses text at entry time, stores in existing fields

### What Makes CheckList Different

The **template-session separation** remains genuinely unique. No competitor offers this. Combined with:
- Encrypted sync (vs plaintext everywhere else)
- True offline-first (vs cached-with-limitations)
- Infinite nesting (vs flat lists)
- No ads (vs ad-supported free tiers)

The gap to close is **friction reduction** (search, smart input), not feature parity with PM tools.

## Target Audience

### Primary
- **Organization-focused users** who want hierarchical templates and clean separation of concerns
- **Families** wanting true real-time collaboration
- **Multi-store shoppers** who maintain different templates per store
- **Privacy-aware users** who prefer encrypted sync over plaintext

### Secondary
- **Meal preppers** who reuse weekly shopping templates
- **Teams/households** needing controlled sharing with permissions
- **Power users** frustrated with flat list limitations

## Competitive Positioning

### Honest Tagline Options
- "Templates that stay clean"
- "Shop from templates, not lists"
- "Organize your shopping, your way"
- "Lists that work offline"

### Value Proposition

> CheckList separates your shopping templates from your shopping sessions—so you can reuse your carefully organized lists week after week without resetting anything. Combined with real-time collaboration, encrypted sync, and true offline support, it's the shopping list app for people who take organization seriously.

### Key Messages

1. **Templates**: "Create once, shop forever. Your templates stay clean while sessions track your progress."

2. **Organization**: "Nested folders, nested categories. Organize your shopping the way your brain works."

3. **Reliability**: "Works in airplane mode, underground, or anywhere with spotty reception."

4. **Collaboration**: "Share folders with family. See updates instantly. No one buys duplicates."

5. **Privacy**: "Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. No ads, no tracking."
