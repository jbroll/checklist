## Top 10 Finalists (My Recommendations)

### Gold Tier (Most Likely to Succeed)

1. **Lyova** - Flows off tongue, implies collaboration, feminine-friendly, globally neutral. Sounds like a place or person but completely invented.

2. **Kavio** - Sharp, professional, tech-adjacent without being generic tech. Memorably pronounces KAY-vee-oh.

3. **Navio** - Navigator energy, implies real-time guidance. Easy to spell after hearing. Strong brand presence.

4. **Rixo** - Modern, minimal. Could easily become a logo mark (Rx + O aesthetic). Very memorable.

5. **Zexo** - Futuristic, energetic. Z is underutilized in naming. Feels premium like Zillow.

### Silver Tier (Strong Alternatives)

6. **Velix** - Sophisticated, implies velocity/movement. Sounds like a real tech company already.

7. **Klixt** - Ultra-modern, implies clicking/organizing. Tech-native feel.

8. **Molix** - Smooth, scientific sounding, implies fluidity of data.

9. **Mivar** - Flexible, musical, approachable. Could work in any language.

10. **Levio** - Lighthearted but serious. Implies ease of use. Harry Potter fans might smile.

## Naming Rationale by Choice

### Why These Work:

**Lyova**:
- Easier pronunciation than invented tech brands
- Implies "liquid" + "ova" (origin) = real-time source
- Feminine without being cutesy
- Works across Romance, Germanic, and Slavic languages

**Kavio**:
- K opening = sharp, trustworthy, tech-forward
- Flows like Mario, Patio, Studio (proven naming patterns)
- Implies motion, direction, navigation
- Easy to trademark (no conflicts)

**Navio**:
- "Nav" = navigation, but "Navio" feels like a proper noun
- Similar DNA to Figma, Canva (accessible brands)
- Could become a cute mascot (ship/navigation theme)
- Strong domain availability

**Rixo**:
- Minimum letters, maximum impact
- "Rix" = organize/arrange (implied)
- Could have "Rx" pharmaceutical-grade precision association
- Would look great as simple wordmark

**Zexo**:
- Z = modern, energetic, different
- Hex/exponential growth implied
- Feels like Xerox, Zendesk level of brand authority
- Phonetically satisfying

## Implementation Suggestion

**For decision-making**, narrow to your top 5, then:

1. **Say them out loud** - Does it feel natural when spoken?
2. **Get team feedback** - What's your emotional reaction?
3. **Search trademark databases** - Verify clean legal standing
4. **Mock up logos** - How does it look in text/icon form?
5. **Test with users** - "Quick! Name this app!" game with outsiders

**Personal pick**: **Navio** - It's distinctive, memorable, internationally friendly, and carries implications of guidance/navigation that fit your real-time, collaborative vision.

**Runner-up**: **Lyova** - If you want something that feels more approachable and less "tech brand" and more "lifestyle brand"

---

## Avoid These (Why They Don't Work)

- Anything with obvious real-word components (immediately feels lazy)
- Single letter + numbers (looks like passwords, not brands)
- Excessive silent letters (harder to spell)
- Sounds like medical conditions or curse words in any language
- Too many syllables (Kjekit is already unique; keep it 2 syllables max)
